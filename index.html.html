Quick answers

Are you really open 24 hours?

Google lists the clinic as open 24 hours. Specialist availability can differ by time of day, so please call 090 337 3737 to confirm before you travel.

What kind of care does an internal medicine clinic provide?

Internal medicine generally covers the diagnosis and care of adult health conditions. For the exact services at this clinic, please call and ask.

Is this the right place for an emergency?

For a life-threatening emergency, such as severe chest pain, trouble breathing or loss of consciousness, go to the nearest emergency department or call emergency services right away.

How do I ask for a visit?

Use the request form on this page to send an SMS, or call 090 337 3737. Please keep private medical details out of text messages.

What should I bring?

See the "What to bring" checklist in the Plan your visit section. Previous test results and a list of your current medicines are especially helpful.
